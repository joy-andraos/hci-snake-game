HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD

Use arrow keys to move. Press ENTER to start / restart.

GESTURES

Point your index finger in the direction you want to move.

  • Finger pointing up
  • Finger pointing down
  • Finger pointing left
  • Finger pointing right

Make sure only your hand is visible to the camera in good lighting. Set game speed to SLOW for best accuracy.

VOICE

Say UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT clearly.


Speak clearly and set game speed to SLOW for best accuracy.

POSE

Move your whole body to steer the snake.

  • Raise both arms up wide
  • Lower body outside the frame (squat)
  • Extend right arm sideways
  • Extend left arm sideways

Stand back from the camera and in good lighting. Set game speed to SLOW for best accuracy.
